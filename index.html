About

I'm an aerospace engineer working on guidance, navigation, and control for commercial airplanes — the systems that determine where an aircraft is, where it's heading, and how it gets there safely.

My work spans flight control law design, sensor fusion and state estimation, and the modeling and simulation that has to hold up long before anything leaves the ground.
