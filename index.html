About

I'm an aerospace engineer working on the systems that determine where an aircraft is, where it's heading, and how it gets there safely. I spent most of my Boeing career working on the autopilot control laws and flight director guidance for the 787 and 777X airplanes. These are wide body airplanes with a triplex fly-by-wire flight controls system. Lots of time spent on redundancy management and control law robustness. I've also been able to dip my toes in the federated flight controls architectures of the 747, 757, and 767 airplanes. Lots of unique challenges for both types of systems. My expertise is in pitch control laws, pretty much all of them, though I spent the most time and effort on autoland flare. It's unbelievably challenging to design a flare that performs to a very high level, meets all of its stability requirements, AND looks good to the pilot. I'm grateful to have had the opportunity to work on it. More recently, I'm working on developing what's next for flight guidance systems and I wish I could talk more about it.
